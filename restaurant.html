Our story

More than just a restaurant

Welcome to UKitch, where every meal is crafted to bring people together. Founded in 2010 by a group of food and vibe lovers, our journey began with a simple mission: creating unforgettable dining experiences through exceptional food, warm hospitality, matching aesthetics and memorable moments.

Whether you're joining us for breakfast,a group brunch, a business coffee, a family celebration, or a romantic dinner, our carefully curated menu offers something for everyone. From vegan delights to grilled specialties, every dish is prepared with passion and attention to detail.

Today, UKitch serves thousands of guests annually and continues to grow while maintaining the personal touch that made us a local favorite.
